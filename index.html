XL-07 / Typographic portrait

Inner Weather

Words become contour lines. A face emerges through density and pressure, with no portrait photograph or drawn facial boundary.

Composition & materials

Cartography + typographic portraiture + a single blue ink. Authored HTML/CSS/SVG with AI-assisted art direction and code. No client commission is implied.

XL-08 / Couture invitation

Tension Studies

An invitation to an imagined couture presentation. The collection title is cut from an asymmetric field of thread, held between three tension points.

Composition & materials

Textile drape + negative-space lettering + invitation typography. Authored HTML/CSS/SVG with AI-assisted art direction and code. No client commission is implied.

XL-09 / Book jacket

Palimpsest

A complete front, spine and back jacket. Original prose is interrupted by erasure; what survives carries a second, quieter message.

Composition & materials

Literary prose + erasure + imperfect print registration. Authored HTML/CSS/SVG with AI-assisted art direction and code. No client commission is implied.

XL-10 / Record sleeve

Unheard

An imagined experimental recording. The title is an absence in an oblique field of engraved sound traces, with a complete fictional track list.

Composition & materials

Sound engraving + type through absence + record-sleeve information. Authored HTML/CSS/SVG with AI-assisted art direction and code. No client commission is implied.

XL-11 / Exhibition composition

Faultline

An imagined ceramics exhibition. Chalky planes break to reveal cobalt interiors; title fragments bridge the gaps without losing the reading.

Composition & materials

Ceramic strata + fractured typography + museum information. Authored HTML/CSS/SVG with AI-assisted art direction and code. No client commission is implied.

XL-12 / Beverage label

Night Orchard

A flat label for an imagined pomegranate botanical drink. Engraved skin gives way to luminous seed chambers, surrounded by usable product information.

Composition & materials

Botanical engraving + seed translucency + packaging hierarchy. Authored HTML/CSS/SVG with AI-assisted art direction and code. No client commission is implied.

XL-13 / Architectural poster

Borrowed Sun

An imagined paper-and-light exhibition. An impossible window is built from a few cut planes; a shadow extends the title into the room.

Composition & materials

Paper relief + directional light + architectural typography. Authored HTML/CSS/SVG with AI-assisted art direction and code. No client commission is implied.

XL-14 / Dance poster

Body Electric

A fictional contemporary-dance programme. Variable-width strokes establish balance, limbs and momentum without a continuous body outline.

Composition & materials

Calligraphic gesture + expressive type + programme hierarchy. Authored HTML/CSS/SVG with AI-assisted art direction and code. No client commission is implied.

XL-15 / Film key art

The Last Postcard

Key art for an imagined film. A quiet coastline and an unfilled address let the absent recipient occupy more space than the landscape.

Composition & materials

Coastal stippling + postal ephemera + literary restraint. Authored HTML/CSS/SVG with AI-assisted art direction and code. No client commission is implied.

XL-16 / Cultural campaign

Common Futures

An imagined cultural campaign. Cut, folded, overprinted and stitched paper pieces form a word that is both image and invitation.

Composition & materials

Paper construction + overprint + deliberately irregular lettering. Authored HTML/CSS/SVG with AI-assisted art direction and code. No client commission is implied.

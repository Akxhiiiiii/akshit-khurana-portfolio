Fragrance · Unofficial brand study

SUGAR HAS A SHADOW

Petal sweetness with a deeper afterimage.

The idea

The first image places sugared petals against a darkening form; the second lets the floral signal recede so the woods and vanilla stay in view.

Making the world

The pair uses softness and shadow as one material progression. It is an independent visual concept, with generated bottle details subject to packshot review.

The series

Project status

This is an unofficial, self-initiated study using Billie Eilish Fragrances for identification. It is not commissioned, approved or endorsed by the brand. Images are AI-assisted art-direction visualisations. Product geometry, small packaging text and rights need verification before official or commercial production.

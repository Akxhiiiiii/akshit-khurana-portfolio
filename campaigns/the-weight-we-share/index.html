THE WEIGHT WE SHARE

Strength as a load carried together.

The idea

Two taut ropes make the pressure on a monumental stone form legible. The pair moves from held weight to an opening, with the amber bottle calm at the centre.

Making the world

Rope fibre, stone fracture and warm glass remain consistent across two different camera distances. The generated package details are concept-level, not production packshots.

The series

Project status

This is an unofficial, self-initiated study using Emporio Armani for identification. It is not commissioned, approved or endorsed by the brand. Images are AI-assisted art-direction visualisations. Product geometry, small packaging text and rights need verification before official or commercial production.

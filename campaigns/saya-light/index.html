Objects · Fictional identity

SĀYA

A collectible light sculpture that changes the room when it opens.

The idea

Desire becomes a private act of discovery. Two blackened-bronze forms part to reveal a smoked amber core; the campaign moves from a nearly closed silhouette to an illuminated room and then into the material itself.

Making the world

Bronze patina, cast-glass inclusions and a narrow amber reflection create the sequence. This is a fictional object shown through AI-assisted studies; its pivot, wiring and heat management have not been engineered or manufactured.

The series

Project status

This is a self-initiated fictional brand and campaign concept. No real product launch or client commission is implied. Images are AI-assisted art-direction visualisations. Product geometry, small packaging text and rights need verification before official or commercial production.

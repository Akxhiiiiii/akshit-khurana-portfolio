COLD / CONTACT

What if cold left a temporary print?

The idea

A freezer-cold bottle becomes a printing block. Its frost ghost carries the silhouette into a poster language that can outlive the moment of contact.

Making the world

The green bottle and orange signal stay recognizable; the irregular pale imprint does the new graphic work. This remains a visual study, with packaging microtype requiring verification.

Project status

This is an unofficial, self-initiated study using Jägermeister for identification. It is not commissioned, approved or endorsed by the brand. Intended for an adult audience. Images are AI-assisted art-direction visualisations. Product geometry, small packaging text and rights need verification before official or commercial production.

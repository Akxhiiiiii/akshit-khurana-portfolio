Fragrance · Unofficial brand study

SLOW UNFURLING

Warm spice and woods translated into an unfolding space.

The idea

Rather than line up fragrance notes as props, the campaign makes warmth architectural: amber light moves through folds, then settles into a last ember.

Making the world

Cinnamon, dates, vanilla and amber inform colour and atmosphere. The Arabic and fine package details in the generated render need verification.

The series

Project status

This is an unofficial, self-initiated study using Lattafa for identification. It is not commissioned, approved or endorsed by the brand. Images are AI-assisted art-direction visualisations. Product geometry, small packaging text and rights need verification before official or commercial production.

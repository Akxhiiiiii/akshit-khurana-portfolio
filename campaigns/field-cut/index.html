Drinks · Fictional identity

FIELD / CUT

A cane spirit built around the decisive angle of a harvest cut.

The idea

The diagonal cut is the whole system. It opens the set, crosses the paper label and directs light through the amber liquid.

Making the world

Pressed-fibre paper, exposed cane and mineral-black surfaces give the bottle a physical origin. The image tests a packaging and campaign direction, not a finished manufactured product.

Project status

This is a self-initiated fictional brand and campaign concept. No real product launch or client commission is implied. Intended for an adult audience. Images are AI-assisted art-direction visualisations. Product geometry, small packaging text and rights need verification before official or commercial production.

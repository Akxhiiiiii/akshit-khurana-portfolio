THE SOFT CENTRE

A hard-edged bottle inside an almost weightless floral world.

The idea

The bottle’s dark studded form gives the image its tension. Jasmine and pink ranunculus surround it; the second view enters the petals instead of repeating the hero.

Making the world

Translucent organza, diffused rose light and pale stone keep the softness luminous rather than sugary. Packaging details require verification before commercial use.

The series

Project status

This is an unofficial, self-initiated study using Valentino for identification. It is not commissioned, approved or endorsed by the brand. Images are AI-assisted art-direction visualisations. Product geometry, small packaging text and rights need verification before official or commercial production.

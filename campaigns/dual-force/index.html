DBA / DUAL FORCE

A matte-black performance drink shaped by opposing forces.

The idea

Football precision and fight-ring tension meet in one tactile can. Pressed rings carry energy through the packaging and become physical scenery in the campaign.

Making the world

Titanium lettering, mineral-black grain and one broken red-to-gold line unify the arena, sliced-can construction and citrus pour. The product and imagined celebrity pairing are fictional; no endorsement is implied.

The series

Project status

This is a self-initiated fictional brand and campaign concept. No real product launch or client commission is implied. Images are AI-assisted art-direction visualisations. Product geometry, small packaging text and rights need verification before official or commercial production.

VEIL/03: Wear the Afterimage

Make the words part of the image.

Fragrance / Physical typography / Editorial

View 4 applications ↓

The communication problem

Describe an elusive scent without falling back on ornamental luxury conventions or empty atmospheric copy.

Audience & direction

Independent-fragrance buyers drawn to experimental fashion, material photography and limited releases.

Words become light, reflection and residue across the body. A severe product grid turns that physical interruption into a usable identity.

Typography

Wide uppercase grotesk projected at oblique angles, balanced by restrained mono specifications.

Colour

Smoke black, mirror silver, skin warmth and afterimage coral.

Composition

Three stages move from obscured language to clarity; product pages return to square, exact alignments.

Image making

Fashion portraiture, projected light, reflected glass, translucent vinyl and a smoky bottle still life.

Mixture formula

35% body and product photography + 30% physically behaving type + 20% glass distortion + 15% clinical information design.

Selected alternate / Rejected direction

The rejected route placed clean type over the portrait. It advertised a fragrance but did not embody one. The final sequence bends the words through light, skin and reflection so the message has a physical consequence.

Method study: language becomes physical matter and the body becomes the communication surface. The approach transforms a broad principle associated with Stefan Sagmeister’s work without copying his lettering, AIGA work or injury imagery.

AI production disclosure

Portrait and bottle base plates were generated with AI. Lettering was separately authored and composed to behave as projected light and reflected material. No existing Sagmeister composition was copied.

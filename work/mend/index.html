01 / Communication problem

Make a neighbourhood repair cooperative look trustworthy, useful and contemporary while explaining what can be fixed and how to participate.

Residents who own repairable clothes, bags and small furniture but need a clear local alternative to disposal.

Deep teal establishes trust; short repair stitches and red tape make the act of fixing visible without turning it into nostalgia.

Typography

Plain, heavy headings for street legibility and mono labels for service details.

Colour

Workshop teal, repair red, thread ochre and utility cream.

Composition

Modular information panels, visible seams and labels designed for low-cost one- or two-colour printing.

Image making

Vector stitches, repair tape, service forms and material tags.

Mixture formula

45% civic information design + 25% repair marks + 20% packaging logic + 10% workshop texture.

Selected alternate / Rejected direction

A useful no.

The rejected identity used a needle icon as the primary mark, which narrowed the service to clothing. The stitch system can represent many categories and functions as both symbol and divider.

AI production disclosure

The cooperative, services, addresses and schedules are fictional. Identity, copy, diagrams and applications were authored for this concept.

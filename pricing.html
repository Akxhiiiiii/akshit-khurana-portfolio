Project details

What’s included.

Each package includes its stated number of final visuals within one cohesive creative direction, one approved master format and one adapted crop per visual, plus one consolidated revision round for the set.

Final JPG/PNG dimensions and intended use are recorded in the project quote. Standard scope covers the client’s own website, organic social posts and paid digital ads. Print and outdoor production, extra formats, animation, editable files, exclusivity and new creative directions are quoted separately.

Projects typically take up to 14 days, depending on scope. The agreed schedule begins after the brief, required assets and 50% booking payment are received. The remaining 50% is due before final file delivery; delayed feedback or assets may move the delivery date.

AI-assisted methods may be used and will be disclosed. Clients must have permission to use any material they supply. The quote confirms usage, delivery and booking details before work starts.
